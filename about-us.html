Brand History

Forma started as a "what if," What if there are shirts that can accommodate the Philippine climate?
Not the usual heavyweight tees and massive prints, but one that is breathable, light, and fit for the on-the-go.
That's when the team started to dive deep to find the answer and built Forma.
